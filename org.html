    The three human gates

    Everything else runs on its own.

    • 1
      New data buyersThe first delivery to any new buyer, and any new contract or price, needs a person to approve it.
    • 2
      Money out above the capRewards and gift cards pay out automatically up to $500 a week. Anything above waits for approval.
    • 3
      Privacy flagsWhen the Privacy guard blocks something and can't fix it alone, a person decides.

    Runs alone: research, outreach drafts, posts, student support, reports to existing buyers, offer matching, payouts under the cap. Sent by the team: outreach to student groups and social posts.

    Where the data goes

    The Data and offers router knows which buyer gets what, and when. Targeting happens inside SNARE: buyers choose a group of students, SNARE shows the offer, and buyers only get counts back.

    1 · CollectStudents who opted in

    Receipts, weekly check ins, used and tossed items. No names leave the app.

    2 · AnalyseInsights analyst

    Turns events into totals: what gets wasted, when, and why.

    3 · CheckPrivacy guard

    Every number covers 50+ students. Consent checked. Blocks or merges anything smaller.

    4 · RouteData and offers router

    Matches each insight to the buyers who pay for it, and each offer to a student group.

    5 · DeliverReports and in app offers

    Reports go to brands, grocers and the city. Offers show inside SNARE.

    Golden Crust wants to reach students who throw out bread.

    The brand never gets a list of students. Only students who turned on anonymous sharing are matched. Offers are always labelled Sponsored and never change meal ranking or the cheapest trip.
